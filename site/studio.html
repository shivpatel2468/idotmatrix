The studio

Pick an app and it's on the panel in a second. Every setting is live, every form is generated from the app's own schema, and it works the same in a desktop browser and on your phone. The engine serves it at http://127.0.0.1:8765.

Play mode

An arcade on 1,024 pixels

Press ▶ Play on any game. B opens the match setup: mode, players, map and theme. For multiplayer the panel shows a QR code and friends' phones become controllers over your Wi-Fi.

On your phone

The same studio in your pocket, and seven controllers

The studio is responsive, so your phone gets the full library and every setting. In a game, a phone becomes a controller: d-pad, analog stick, swipe, tap zones, tilt, a real gamepad or the keyboard. Friends join with the room code; they only ever reach the controller, never your studio.

  • Seat 1 on the keyboard or the studio, everyone else on phones
  • Empty seats are played by the AI, so a match always starts
  • Every game plays itself as a screensaver until someone presses a key

The 3D Fly view

Watch a fruit-fly brain play

When the Fly Brain plays a game, the studio shows its visual-to-motor circuit in 3D: the panel's frame goes in like a compound eye, neurons fire, and the motor neurons steer. It is only ever shown in the studio, never on the panel.
