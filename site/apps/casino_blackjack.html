Try it

render a frame to PNG (no panel needed)
$ uv run deskdot preview casino_blackjack --settings '{"view":"demo"}' --out casino_blackjack.png
show it on your panel (engine running)
$ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/apps/casino_blackjack/activate

Or ask your agent: “Show Blackjack on my panel”. It calls show_app with "casino_blackjack". New here? Set up DeskDot first.
