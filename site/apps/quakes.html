Try it

render a frame to PNG (no panel needed)
$ uv run deskdot preview quakes --settings '{"feed":"all_hour"}' --out quakes.png
show it on your panel (engine running)
$ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/apps/quakes/activate

Or ask your agent: “Show Earthquakes on my panel”. It calls show_app with "quakes". New here? Set up DeskDot first.
