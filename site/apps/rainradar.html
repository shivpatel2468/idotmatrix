Try it

render a frame to PNG (no panel needed)
$ uv run deskdot preview rainradar --settings '{"zoom":"7"}' --out rainradar.png
show it on your panel (engine running)
$ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/apps/rainradar/activate

Or ask your agent: “Show Rain Radar on my panel”. It calls show_app with "rainradar". New here? Set up DeskDot first.
