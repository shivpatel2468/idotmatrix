Try it

render a frame to PNG (no panel needed)
$ uv run deskdot preview camera --settings '{"rotate":"90"}' --out camera.png
show it on your panel (engine running)
$ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/apps/camera/activate

Or ask your agent: “Show Camera Mirror on my panel”. It calls show_app with "camera". New here? Set up DeskDot first.
