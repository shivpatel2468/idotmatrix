Tides & Surf

Tide curve with a now marker and next high/low, plus wave height, period and direction.

Put it on your panel View source

Try it

render a frame to PNG (no panel needed)
$ uv run deskdot preview tides --settings '{"layout":"surf"}' --out tides.png
show it on your panel (engine running)
$ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/apps/tides/activate

Or ask your agent: “Show Tides & Surf on my panel”. It calls show_app with "tides". New here? Set up DeskDot first.
