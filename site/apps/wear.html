What to Wear

One big icon for the next hours — umbrella, jacket, shades, T-shirt, scarf or snow boots.

Put it on your panel View source

Try it

render a frame to PNG (no panel needed)
$ uv run deskdot preview wear --settings '{"icon":"umbrella"}' --out wear.png
show it on your panel (engine running)
$ curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/apps/wear/activate

Or ask your agent: “Show What to Wear on my panel”. It calls show_app with "wear". New here? Set up DeskDot first.
