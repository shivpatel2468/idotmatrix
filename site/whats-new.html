v3.5.0Housie, the TV view and a high-roller casino7 Oct 2026Latest

New 10

  • Housie (Tambola)

    Beforethe casino had nine tables and no number game.

    Nowa tenth table — buy tickets, the caller draws 1 to 90, your phone marks the numbers and you claim Early Five, the lines, Four Corners or Full House. The house checks every claim.

  • Penguin Escape

    Beforethere was no puzzle game made for the panel.

    Nowhelp a penguin slide across the ice and out of the zoo in 16 levels — push ice blocks, find keys, collect fish and stay out of the keepers' torch light.

  • TV view

    Beforeonly the small LED panel showed the game; everyone had to crowd around it.

    Nowopen the game on any TV, projector or tablet browser in full HD. Casino tables are drawn in full with every player's chips, and the TV shows the same spin, cards and result as the panel at the same moment. Press the gold TV button under the panel to get the link.

  • Chips for high-roller tables

    Beforethe chip rack was always 1, 5, 25, 100 and 500, even when the table minimum was much higher.

    Nowbig tables get 1K, 2K, 5K and more, up to 1M, each in its own colour. Chips the table doesn't allow are not shown at all — on phones, the laptop and the TV alike.

  • More roulette bets with a mouse or finger

    Beforeyou could only drop a chip on a single number or an outside bet.

    Nowdrop or click a chip on the line between two numbers, the corner where four meet, the edge of a row or between two rows. The numbers your bet covers light up while you aim.

  • 21 casino table themes

    Beforeseven plain felt colours.

    Now21 themes, each with its own felt pattern — from Royal and Neon strip to Sakura night, Galaxy and Pixel arcade. The host's theme colours every phone, the laptop and the TV at once.

  • Andar Bahar: exact card option

    Beforea card won when its value matched (any suit).

    Nowthe host can also pick "exact card" — same value and same suit.

  • Coin Gate intro

    Beforethe studio opened with a sunset or circuit scene.

    Nowyou can also pick an arcade cabinet — a silver coin drops in and a heavy gate grinds open.

  • Sounds in the studio

    Beforethe studio was silent.

    Nowsoft clicks, chip sounds, the roulette ball, card deals and more, with an on/off switch and volume for each kind of sound.

  • Casino button everywhere

    Beforeyou had to find a casino table in the app list.

    Nowa gold Casino button sits in the top bar on every screen (with a little coin drop), and the Casino group in the app list takes you straight to the tables.

Better 8

  • Messages you can't miss

    Before"Table min is …" or "Not enough credits" showed small at the top of the phone, and the host on the laptop saw nothing at all.

    Nowthe message pops up big in the middle of the screen on every device.

  • Phone top bar

    Beforeyour credits could run into your picture and the buttons.

    Nowa cleaner bar with your name and credits on their own plaque; big balances shorten (1,250,000 → 1.25M).

  • Everyone in sync

    Beforephones, the laptop and the TV could count down and show results a moment apart.

    Nowevery device follows one shared clock, so countdowns end and results appear at the same moment everywhere.

  • The panel on every phone

    Beforephones only saw the betting table.

    Nowduring the spin or the deal, every phone shows the live panel too.

  • Smoother on a Fire TV

    Beforeanimations on slow TV browsers stuttered.

    Nowheavy effects are gone, and a lighter mode switches on by itself on slow TVs. Games show as a sharp HD picture of the panel (press select on the remote for the classic LED look).

  • The DeskDot logo

    Beforethe old logo in three colours.

    Nowan animated LED logo in two colours — pink "Desk", gold "Dot" — on every screen.

  • Intro previews

    Beforeyou had to pick an intro to find out what it looked like.

    Noweach intro shows a small moving preview under its name in Settings. The OG intro was redrawn: clean, smooth and without glare.

  • Theme picker always available

    Beforetable themes only appeared once a table was running.

    Nowyou can pick a theme any time.

Fixed 2

  • Betting from a laptop as a guest

    Beforeclicking chips with a mouse was laggy or did nothing, and dragging was jumpy.

    Nowone click places one chip, and dragging follows the mouse smoothly.

  • Leaf Leap and Dig World with phones

    Beforea phone joining these games crashed them.

    Nowfriends can join normally.

v3.4.0The casino, and DeskDot in your browser4 Oct 2026

New 6

  • Casino mode

    BeforeDeskDot only had arcade games and apps.

    Nownine casino tables — Roulette, 7 Up 7 Down, Blackjack, Baccarat, Slots, Texas Hold'em, Teen Patti, Andar Bahar and the Big Six wheel. Friends join with their phones and bet with free play credits (they have no money value). Every result can be checked as fair from a phone.

  • Rock Paper Scissors

    Beforenot available.

    Nowplay the computer, a friend, or a tournament of up to 8 players.

  • DeskDot in the browser

    Beforeyou had to install DeskDot on a computer to use it.

    Nowopen the web app and connect to your panel straight from the browser — nothing to install.

  • Play with friends over the internet

    Beforefriends had to be on the same Wi-Fi.

    Nowin the web app, friends can join from anywhere by scanning the code.

  • Camera, screen and music in the browser

    BeforeCamera Mirror, Screen Mirror and the music visualizer only worked in the installed app.

    Nowthey work in the web app too, after the browser asks your permission.

  • How to play and rulebook

    Beforeyou had to know the casino rules already.

    Nowevery table has a "?" with how to play, the full rules and the payouts, plus a short tour the first time.

Better 3

  • Phone version of the studio

    Beforethe studio was a squeezed-down desktop page on phones.

    Nowit works like a phone app — a tab bar at the bottom, one screen per tab and sheets that slide up.

  • Casino previews

    Beforethe casino previews were still pictures.

    Nowthey play a whole demo round.

  • Panel while waiting for bets

    Beforethe panel kept showing the join code during a game.

    Nowonce someone sits down, it shows the table and who has placed their chips.

Fixed 1

  • Apps in the browser

    Beforesome apps failed quietly in the web app.

    Noweach one was checked; the ones that can't work in a browser say so and explain why.

v3.3.0The fruit fly, and a new name2 Oct 2026

New 5

  • A fruit fly that plays your games

    Beforegames played themselves with a simple computer player.

    Nowa model of a real fruit fly's brain watches the panel and plays — and you can watch its brain light up in 3D next to the game.

  • Colour calibration

    Beforecolours on the panel could look different from the screen.

    Nowa guided test matches the panel's colours to your screen.

  • Intro and outro scenes

    Beforethe studio just appeared.

    Nowa short animated intro plays while it connects — Sunset, Circuit or the original LED fly-in.

  • Search bar for your computer

    Beforeyou had to open the studio to change what the panel shows.

    Nowpress Ctrl+Alt+Space anywhere and type.

  • Join with your phone

    Beforephones were plain controllers.

    Nowpick your name, colour and character, see who else is in, and get your seat back if you drop out.

Better 3

  • New name: DeskDot

    Beforethe project was called DotDeck.

    Nowit's DeskDot everywhere. Your old settings still work.

  • Now playing

    Beforemusic info was a small card.

    Nowa full-screen now-playing view, like a music player.

  • New website

    Beforeone long page.

    Nowseparate pages with a live preview of every app.

Fixed 2

  • Panel reconnects after sleep

    Beforeafter the computer slept, the panel could stay disconnected.

    NowDeskDot notices and reconnects by itself.

  • Calendar after midnight

    Beforea meeting in progress just after midnight disappeared from the timeline.

    Nowit shows.

v3.2.0First public release1 Oct 2026

New 3

  • 22 games with friends

    Beforea handful of single-player games.

    Now22 games for 1 to 4 players — versus, co-op, free-for-all and teams — with maps and themes to pick from.

  • Phones as controllers

    Beforeyou played with the keyboard only.

    Nowthe panel shows a code, friends scan it and their phones become controllers, with seven layouts to choose from.

  • Android app (beta)

    BeforeDeskDot needed a computer running.

    Nowa spare Android phone can run it all day instead.

Better 2

  • Smoother, clearer panel

    Beforesome animations stepped or jumped, and small details were hard to see.

    Nowevery app was reworked to move smoothly and read clearly on the real panel.

  • Game settings in the studio

    Beforesettings were one long list.

    Nowthey're grouped into Game, Graphics and Game flow, and you set up a match before you play.

Fixed 1

  • Privacy

    Beforeyour panel's address was stored in the code.

    Nowit's kept only in your own settings file, and secrets are hidden everywhere.
