v3.8.0Install DeskDot like any app, and update it from inside29 Oct 2026Latest

Version 3.8.0 on GitHub · Download DeskDot

New 4

  • Installers for Windows, Mac and Android

    Beforeto use DeskDot you had to download the code, install Python tools and type commands in a terminal.

    Nowdownload one file from GitHub — a setup program for Windows, a disk image for Mac, an app for Android — and install it like any other app. DeskDot opens the studio in your browser and sits in the tray, ready to go.

  • Updates from inside DeskDot

    Beforeto get a new version you had to go to GitHub, download it and set it up again yourself.

    Nowwhen a new version is out, a small "Update available" sign appears at the top. Settings → Device → Updates shows what's new in plain words, and one button downloads it, checks it's genuine and installs it. On a computer DeskDot restarts by itself; on Android the phone asks you to confirm. Your settings stay.

  • You choose whether DeskDot looks for updates

    Beforethere was nothing to check.

    Nowabout every 6 hours DeskDot asks GitHub if there's a new version. Nothing about you is sent. Turn it off in Settings → Device → Updates, or press Check now whenever you like.

  • Start DeskDot when you sign in (Windows)

    Beforeyou had to start DeskDot yourself every time.

    Nowtick one box in the installer (or "Start with login" in the tray menu) and it starts with Windows.

Better 1

  • Your settings live in one safe place

    Beforesettings and data sat inside the DeskDot folder, next to the program.

    Nowthe installed app keeps them in your own user folder, so updating or reinstalling never touches them.

v3.7.0Page themes everywhere and a luxury casino on the TV12 Oct 2026

    Nowthey take the page theme's colours too, just like casino games take their table's colours — tuned so they stay bright and readable on the LEDs.   • You can see who won

    Beforewhen a round ended it was hard to tell who had won.

    Nowa big arcade moment plays on the TV — WINNER!, BIG WIN!, MEGA WIN! or JACKPOT! slams onto the screen, gold coins fly into the winner's name plate, which lights up while "+12,500" counts up, and the screen shakes on big wins.   • Your own win on your phone

    Beforeyour phone showed a small "You won" card.

    Nowyour phone shows the same JACKPOT / MEGA WIN / BIG WIN moment for your own result, with spinning rays, a counter, a coin shower and a buzz pattern.

    Beforethe marker on the winning number was always a glass dolly.

    Nowpick one of eight in the casino's House settings — Classic Gold (the default), Crystal, Chrome, Black Onyx & Gold, Rose Gold, Diamond-cut, Marble & Brass or Neon Arcade. On the TV the dealer's hand lifts it, glides it over the table and sets it down on the number with a soft click.

  • Chips that match the table

    Beforeevery table used the same chips.

    Noweach table theme has its own chip design — clay, printed ceramic, gold-rimmed, neon, jade, pixel or ivory — on the TV, the phones and the studio. The colours that tell you a chip's value stay the same. You can also pick one set for every table in House settings.

  • Money that rolls like a slot machine

    Beforecredits just changed to the new number.

    Nowwhen your credits change, the digits spin like slot-machine reels and land on the new amount — on the TV, your phone and the studio.

  • Eight new Now Playing looks

    BeforeNow Playing had 11 layouts.

    Beforeyou chose the Now Playing layout from a list of names.

It works in light and dark and in every page theme, on the phone pages, the web app and the launcher too; the TV gets a calm, still version. - On older or slower phones, inside the Android app, and wherever blur isn't supported, the glass turns frosted — nearly solid, so text never sits on top of busy content. "Reduce transparency" on your device makes it solid. You can choose for yourself in the Display menu → More → Glass (Auto, Clear, Frosted, Solid).
