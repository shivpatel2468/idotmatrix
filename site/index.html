What it is

One engine owns the panel. Everything else talks to it.

DeskDot runs on your computer (or a Raspberry Pi) and keeps the panel's Bluetooth link smooth. You drive it from a studio in your browser, from your phone, from scripts, or by asking an AI agent.

The engine

Renders every app at 32×32, bakes loops into GIFs the panel plays on its own, streams only what changes, and reconnects by itself.

The studio

Library, playlists, presets and live settings with an LED-accurate preview, on desktop and phone. See the studio.

The MCP server

21 tools let Claude and other agents look at the panel, run any app, change settings, draw pixel art and notify you. Set it up.
